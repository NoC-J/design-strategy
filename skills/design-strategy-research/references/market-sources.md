# Where to find local competitors and customer voice

Search the home market's own channels, not only Google. For each market, build this list in step 2 and note which ones you could reach. Add any that are missing for the country.

## Checklist (adapt names to the country)
| Channel | What it shows | How to search |
|---|---|---|
| Local business directories (yellow-pages style) | Who exists, addresses, phones, branches | `<product> <city> directory`, then the directory's own category page |
| Maps listings (Google Maps, Apple Maps, local equivalents) | Real-world presence, reviews, opening hours, photos | `<product> near <city>`, read ratings and review text |
| Facebook pages and groups, Instagram, TikTok | Informal sellers with no website; prices and customer questions often posted openly | `<product> <city> facebook`, group names for farmers, builders, contractors, etc. |
| WhatsApp business catalogues (via links on sites or pages) | How small sellers list sizes and prices | Look for "wa.me" links or "WhatsApp" on competitor pages |
| Local classifieds and marketplaces | Price levels, what buyers ask | `<product> for sale <country>` and the country's classified sites |
| Industry bodies, standards bodies, associations | Who is licensed or certified; what standards buyers expect | `<industry> association <country>`, national standards body certified-supplier lists |
| Tender and procurement portals, government and utility notices | Bulk buyers' requirements, required certifications, who wins | `<utility/agency> tender <product>`, procurement portal listings |
| Local news and trade press | Company news, expansions, supply problems, regulation changes | `<company> <country> news`, trade publications |
| Review sites and forums | Customer complaints, language and objections | `<product> reviews <country>`, local forums, subreddits, community groups |
| Regional equivalents | Neighbouring markets' leaders and exporters | Repeat the above for the nearest 1-2 countries |

## Rules
- Put the country or city in every query. The search tool can skew towards US results.
- Record language(s) used by customers (including local-language terms) and search in them too.
- Record currency and how prices are shown (quoted, per unit, in USD or local currency).
- Note how customers actually buy: phone, WhatsApp, walk-in, tender, marketplace. The site design must support that path.
- A competitor with no website is still a competitor. Profile their listing, social page and reviews, and note the missing site as a finding.
- Treat social pages as public data only. Don't join private groups or message people to gather research.
- If a channel can't be reached (login, blocked host, bot check), say so in the report limits. Don't guess its content.
